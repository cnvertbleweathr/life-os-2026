ONS redesign: Logbook Color chosen

Static mockups of Home and the CFB slate, built from live ONS data captured Nov 3, 2026. Each direction answers the ranked problems in critique.md. Click a column's links to open the full page or its brief. Chosen: Logbook's layout with Pocket's area colours and a light/dark theme (column 6). The theme switch applies to it only.

1 · Almanac II evolved

Keep the masthead and stat band; fix the system around them. Inputs on Today, "needs vs doing" on every goal, no tiny caps. Spectral, Spline Sans, figures in mono.

2 · Ledger new, light

The year as a balance sheet. Colourless chrome, Geist with every figure in mono, one blue on the main data element: where each goal lands by Dec 31.

Open pageBrief

3 · Logbook new, light

The week is the unit. A Mon–Sun strip of lanes (runs, CrossFit, habits, check-in, plans, CFB) on a white sheet in a green-grey frame; Saturday as a kickoff strip. IBM Plex.

Open pageBrief

4 · Pocket new, phone-first

Designed for the thumb. A To-do-today stack of toggles first, then one pale tile per life area with its sum and a coach line. Picks as big readable slips. Manrope.

Open pageBrief

5 · Ops dark terminal

A console for the life pipeline. Goal monitors, a keyboard queue, and a Sources panel that flags stale data. Every selection is one 1px green line. Public Sans with JetBrains Mono figures.

Open pageBrief

6 · Logbook Color chosen

Logbook's week strip and L-frame, with each life area in its own tint (Pocket) and a light/dark switch (Ops). Forest stays the system colour for today, selection and focus.
