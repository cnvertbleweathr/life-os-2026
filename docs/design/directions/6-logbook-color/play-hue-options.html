The eight area hues sit, in OKLCH, at 3° (plans), 48° (CFB), 70° (CrossFit), 139° (habits), 177° (reading), 258° (running and weather) and 290° (check-in). The three widest gaps are at about 105°, 218° and 322°, so each option sits in the middle of one of them. Every option uses the same tint, ink and mark recipe as the existing areas.
